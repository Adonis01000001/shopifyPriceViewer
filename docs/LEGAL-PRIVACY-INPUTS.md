# Legal and Privacy Release Inputs

This is an input sheet, not legal advice and not a substitute for approved legal
documents. It prevents the application team from inventing commercial or privacy
claims.

Before public release, the owner must provide:

- Legal entity name, jurisdiction, and business address.
- Support email, emergency contact email/phone, and response target.
- Approved Terms of Service URL.
- Approved Privacy Policy URL.
- Data controller/processor roles and applicable regions.
- Data categories: account identity, Shopify store metadata, product catalog,
  competitor URLs/prices, analytics events, logs, and billing identifiers.
- Retention periods and deletion/export procedure for each category.
- Subprocessor list and DPAs: Shopify, hosting, PostgreSQL, Redis, Stripe or
  Shopify billing, scraping providers, search providers, AI providers, and SMTP.
- Trial, pricing, tax, refund, cancellation, and failed-payment language.
- Security incident notification owner and escalation path.

The application must not be marked legally ready until these inputs are approved
and linked from the public listing and account surfaces.
